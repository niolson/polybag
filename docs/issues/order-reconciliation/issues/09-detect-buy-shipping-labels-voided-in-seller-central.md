# Detect Buy Shipping Labels voided in Seller Central

Status: ready-for-human
Category: research
Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md), decision 8. Independent of the other slices.

## Problem

A Buy Shipping Label can be voided in Seller Central (confirmed 2026-10-12). PolyBag
does not find out: the Package stays `Shipped` with an active Label that no longer
exists, and End of Day and tracking keep treating it as live. Shopify has
`ShopifyFulfillmentSynchronizer` for this; Amazon has nothing.

## First: a probe

Buy a Label through PolyBag on a real order, void it in Seller Central, and record what
Shipping v2 says afterwards about that shipment:

- `getTracking` for its tracking number
- `getShipmentDocuments` for its shipment ID
- whether `cancelShipment` from PolyBag now errors, and with what
- whether the Orders API's `fulfillment`/`packages` changes

Captures go in `.scratch/`, not here.

## Then

If something reports the void, a scheduled check over Buy Shipping Labels not yet
delivered (alongside `packages:refresh-tracking`, within its existing request budget)
marks them `VoidedUpstream` through the existing void workflow, making the Package
unshipped. If nothing reports it, say so here, and close this `wontfix` with that
finding; `01` at least makes an operator void visible.
