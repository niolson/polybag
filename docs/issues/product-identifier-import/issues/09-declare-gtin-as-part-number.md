# Declare the GTIN as the part number when a client opts in

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md). Independent of the other slices. Ship it before 1 November 2026.

## Problem

`eu-product-identifiers/04` refuses an EU consumer label when a line has no SKU or no
manufacturer part number (NS-PID). Both carriers also send no identifier block at all for
such a line. A product with a valid GTIN but no part number is refused, even though the
Commission's guidance lets a manufacturer use its standardized identifier as the NS-PID
too:

> Also, the manufacturer, producer or product supplier may decide to assign no NS-PID but
> use the S-PID (see below) also as NS-PID to simplify their operations; this is
> perfectly acceptable.

*Importation and exportation of low value consignments – the EUR 3 temporary customs
duty, "Guidance for Member States and Trade"*, European Commission (DG TAXUD), version of
2 June 2026, §3.5.4.

That is the manufacturer's decision, not ours. The same guidance assumes every product
has an NS-PID, expects a manufacturer without one to assign one, and holds the declarant
responsible for the declaration's accuracy (§3.5.5, Article 15 UCC). So PolyBag does not
make this substitution on its own. The client turns it on.

Measured against Amazon catalog records (PRD), this covers 627 of 633 products in one
health-and-beauty seller's catalog and 451 of 600 in a broad category sample. Shopify
variants and hand-entered products with a barcode benefit equally.

## Desired behavior

- **The setting.** It follows the `label_reference_source` pattern: an app-wide default,
  plus a nullable per-client override that falls back to it. A single-client install has
  no client form in the navigation, so it needs the app-wide setting.
  - App setting `declare_gtin_as_part_number`, default off, a toggle in the *Shipping
    Labels* section of `app/Filament/Pages/Settings.php` beside `label_reference_source`.
    A small resolver like `LabelReferenceResolver` answers it for a client.
  - Nullable boolean `clients.declare_gtin_as_part_number`, on the client form's
    *Shipping Labels* section. Choices: *Use app setting (…)*, *Yes*, *No*.
  - Label: *Declare the GTIN as the manufacturer part number*. Helper: "When a product
    has no manufacturer part number, send its GTIN in that field for EU customs.
    Customs accepts this when the manufacturer uses the GTIN as its part number. Turn it
    on only if that is true of this client's products."
- **The substitution.** It happens in `CustomsItem::fromPackageItem()`. When the
  product's `manufacturer_part_number` is blank, the setting resolved for the product's
  client is on, and `standardProductIdFor()` found a GTIN, `manufacturerProductId` is that
  GTIN. A filled `manufacturer_part_number` always wins. A barcode that is not a valid
  GTIN is never used. Nothing is written to the Product, which keeps showing no part
  number.
- **Everything downstream is unchanged.** `ShipRequest::customsItemsMissingProductIdentifiers()`,
  `MissingProductIdentifierException`, and both adapters' identifier blocks read
  `manufacturerProductId` and need no change.
- **The refusal says so.** When a line is refused for a missing part number and its
  product has a valid GTIN but the setting is off, the message adds that turning on
  *Declare the GTIN as the manufacturer part number* would also resolve it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Setting off (default): a product with a GTIN and no part number is refused for an
      EU consumer, as today
- [ ] Setting on for the client: the same product ships, and FedEx `nonStandardManufacturerProductId`
      and UPS identifier `0200` both carry the GTIN
- [ ] A product with its own part number sends that part number with the setting on
- [ ] A product whose barcode is not a valid GTIN, with no `gtin` and no part number, is
      still refused with the setting on
- [ ] A client override of *No* beats an app setting of on, and *Yes* beats off; unset
      follows the app setting
- [ ] The Product's `manufacturer_part_number` stays null after a label is bought
- [ ] The refusal message names the setting only when the product has a valid GTIN
