# Stop packing on a wrong-item scan

Status: done
Category: enhancement
Created: 2026-11-05

## Problem

When a packer scans an item that is not on the order, Scan & Pack shows an error toast
in the corner and carries on. The packer is looking at the product and the box, not the
screen, so the toast is easy to miss. The scan changed nothing on the page, but the item
may already be in the box. Packing validation will block the purchase later if an item
is short, but nothing catches the wrong item that went in. With validation off, or in
scan-to-add mode, nothing checks the packed items at all.

The moment to catch it is the scan itself. The packer should not be able to keep
scanning until they have seen the mistake.

## Current state

- `scanProduct()` in `resources/views/filament/pages/pack.blade.php` sends a danger
  `FilamentNotification` for **Item Not Found** (not on this Shipment) and **Already
  Packed** (every unit already scanned). Scanning continues.
- In scan-to-add mode, `Pack::addItemByScan()` dispatches `scan-to-add-not-found`, which
  shows a **Product Not Found** toast. Scanning continues.
- Unknown box codes, inactive boxes and unknown commands also toast. They put nothing
  wrong in the box, and this issue leaves them alone.
- The page makes no sound. The transparency-code modal is the only thing that currently
  takes over the scan input.
- Commands are `ScanCommand` cases, run in the browser by `executeCommand()`. The command
  sheet (`PrintCommandBarcodes`) lists every case, so a new case appears there without
  further work.

## Proposal

### 1. A blocking overlay for the three item errors

On **Item Not Found**, **Already Packed** and **Product Not Found**, show a full-screen
overlay in place of the toast:

- It is red, high contrast, and readable from a step back from the screen. It names the
  problem and the code scanned, for example *WRONG ITEM: ABC-123 is not on this order*
  or *EXTRA UNIT: every ABC-123 is already packed*, with one line saying what to do:
  take it out of the box.
- It is the primary signal and must work on its own. Many packing stations have no
  speakers, and warehouses are loud.
- An error tone may play too, but only as a secondary signal. Nothing may depend on it
  being heard.

### 2. Scans are swallowed while it is up

While the overlay is up, any scan except the acknowledge command (below) is discarded,
not processed and not queued. The overlay pulses again to show the scan was refused.
Otherwise the packer scans the next item and carries on without looking up, and the
overlay achieves nothing.

### 3. Acknowledging it

The packer can acknowledge it in three ways:

- **Scan `%COK`.** This is a new `ScanCommand` case, `OK`, labeled *OK*, that acts on
  *the open prompt on this page*. Following ADR-0007 decision 4, a page with nothing
  open rejects it ("Nothing to confirm") rather than reinterpreting it.
- **Press Enter on the keyboard, with nothing typed before it.** A keyboard-wedge scanner
  ends every scan with Enter. If any Enter dismissed the overlay, the next scanned item
  would dismiss it too, and that scan's text would be lost. Only an Enter with an empty
  input buffer counts.
- **Click the overlay's OK button.**

Acknowledging returns focus to the scan input with the packing state unchanged. The
refused scan never changed it.

### 4. Bookkeeping

- Add `%COK` to ADR-0007 decision 4's command table. Stations must reprint their command
  sheets to get it.
- The overlay must coexist with the transparency modal and with a purchase in progress
  (`isShipping`). Neither can be open at the moment of a product scan today, but the
  guards should make that explicit.

## Deferred

- **`%CCANCEL`.** A mis-scan has nothing to cancel. A Cancel command is worth adding
  once a second prompt needs a scan-only *no*. The candidates are the shipment chooser
  when a scan matches several Shipments, and a confirmation before `%CVOID`, which today
  voids immediately.
- **Louder feedback for the non-item errors** (unknown box or command). These are
  candidates for an error tone or a larger toast, but they do not interrupt packing.
- **A setting to turn the overlay off.** Add one only if packers push back.
- **Ship and Manual Ship.** They do not scan items, so this issue is limited to
  Scan & Pack.

## Testing

- A Pest browser test (`tests/Browser/`) covering:
  - a wrong-item scan shows the overlay;
  - a following product scan is swallowed and the packed count does not change;
  - Enter with a non-empty buffer does not dismiss the overlay, but an empty Enter does;
  - scanning `%COK` dismisses it and the next product scan counts.
- Repeat the browser test for Already Packed, and for Product Not Found in scan-to-add
  mode.
- A feature test that `Pack::scanCommandCodes()` includes `%COK` and that the command
  sheet lists it.

  a scan past the ordered quantity can only mean an extra unit in the box, and no
  correction workflow needs it. Moved to `ready-for-agent`.
- 2026-11-05: Done. `ScanCommand::Ok` (`%COK`) and a red full-screen alert on Scan & Pack
  for a wrong item, an extra unit, and an unknown product in scan-to-add mode. While it is
  up, every scan except OK is refused and counted, Buy & Print and F12 are blocked, and a
  short square-wave tone plays when the browser allows it. An empty Enter acknowledges it,
  except within 500 ms of it opening or of a refused scan. A scanner that ends scans with
  CR+LF would otherwise answer the alert with the second Enter of the scan that raised it,
  or of any scan refused later. Review caught the second case, where the window had been
  measured only from the opening. With no alert open,
  OK says "Nothing to Confirm". ADR-0007 decision 4 lists `%COK`. Tests:
  `tests/Browser/PackMisScanTest.php`, plus the command lists in `PackTest` and
  `PrintCommandBarcodesTest`.
