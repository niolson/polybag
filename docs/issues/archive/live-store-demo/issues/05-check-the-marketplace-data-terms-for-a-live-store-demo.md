# Check the marketplace data terms for a live-store demo

Status: done
Category: research
Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md). Blocks connecting a live store to a demo tenant.

## Question

Shopify's protected customer data requirements and Amazon's Data Protection Policy both
limit what customer PII may be used for and how long it may be kept. A merchant trying
PolyBag on their own orders is close to the purpose both allow: fulfilling those orders.
Keeping the data in a sales-demo environment afterwards is weaker ground.

Answer, for each marketplace:

- Is a time-boxed demo on the merchant's own orders, with nothing shipped through it, a
  permitted use?
- What retention limit applies after the demo, and does `04`'s purge meet it?
- Does the demo tenant need to meet the same controls as production? Shopify's level 2
  data protections; Amazon's encryption, access and incident requirements.
- Shopify: is the demo tenant using the same app as production? The app's approved
  protected-data access is what the demo would be relying on.
- Amazon: is it consistent with what has been told to the vendor security review? If the
  review describes the environments PII lives in, a demo tenant holding it must be one of
  them.

## Outcome

Decided 2026-10-22, from Shopify's protected customer data requirements
(shopify.dev, *Protected customer data*, Requirements) and Amazon's Data Protection
Policy (Solution Provider Portal, DPP).

### Shopify: permitted with the merchant's agreement

- **Use.** Level 1 limits processing to "the minimum personal data required to provide
  app functionality to merchants" and to "the stated purposes", and requires "privacy and
  data protection agreements with your merchants". A demo on the merchant's own orders is
  the app's own functionality shown to that merchant, so it is permitted when the
  merchant has agreed to it.
- **Sales-process step.** Before a prospect connects their store, get their written
  agreement: what is read (their open orders, with customer names and addresses), what is
  done with it (shown in the demo; nothing written back, no real labels), and when it is
  deleted (when the demo ends, at most the agreed time limit). A short email the prospect
  confirms is enough; it is the merchant agreement level 1 asks for.
- **Retention.** Level 1 asks for retention periods "to make sure that personal data
  isn't kept for longer than needed". The demo's need ends with the demo, and `04`'s
  teardown deletes the tenant then. `DEMO_EXPIRES_AT` is the period; keep it to what was
  agreed with the merchant.
- **Controls.** A demo tenant holding a live store's orders holds production data, so it
  meets level 2 as production does: encryption at rest and in transit, staff access
  limited to the presenter and the prospect, strong passwords and MFA (`04`), an access
  log, and the incident response policy. With no backups, backup encryption does not
  arise. Level 2's "Keep test and production data separate" is met by the tenant being
  its own environment, holding only that store (`04`).
- **App.** The demo connects through the same Shopify app as production, through our
  OAuth broker, so it relies on that app's approved protected-data access. Confirm the
  app is approved for the fields a demo reads (name, address, phone, email) before the
  first live Shopify demo; Shopify redacts unapproved fields rather than refusing them.

### Amazon: permitted with the PII stripped, as `03` does

- **Use.** The DPP grants PII "for select tax and merchant-fulfilled shipping purposes,
  on a must-have basis", and a demo that ships nothing is neither. So the demo must not
  keep PII. `03` already makes that so: in Demo an imported order keeps only city,
  state, postal code and country, with `Address withheld` as line 1, and no name, phone or
  email, and nothing beyond those fields is logged.
- **Retention.** PII retention is capped at 30 days after order delivery, and other
  Amazon information may not be kept "beyond the scope of its authorized use" (§1.7). The
  demo stores no PII, and the teardown deletes the rest when the demo ends.
- **Controls.** The DPP requires separate test and production environments (§2.5), MFA
  for all user accounts (§1.4.1, with a 12-character minimum where passwords are used;
  `demo:provision` generates 20), and encryption at rest and in transit. A demo tenant
  meets these as any hosted tenant does. The search response still carries the
  recipient's fields in memory until the import strips them, so the tenant must be
  inside the environments described to the vendor security review: a hosted tenant on
  the same infrastructure, not a separate demo host.

### What this means

- A live store may be connected to a demo tenant once the prospect has agreed in writing
  (Shopify) or at all (Amazon, where nothing identifying is kept).
- The time limit is what was agreed with the prospect, and not longer than needed for the
  demo. No marketplace sets a shorter one: Amazon's 30 days applies to PII the demo does
  not keep.
- Open before the first live Shopify demo: confirm the production Shopify app's protected
  customer data approval covers the fields the demo reads.
