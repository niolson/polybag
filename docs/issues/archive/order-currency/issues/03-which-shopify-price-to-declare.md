# Which Shopify price an order declares

Status: done
Category: bug
Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

`order-currency`. Blocked by `01`, which is `done`.

## Problem

The Shopify import values each item at `lineItem.originalUnitPriceSet.shopMoney.amount`
(`ShopifySource`, in both fulfillment order fragments and the item mapping), and the
Shipment's `value` and `currency` from the same field. A probe on a development store
(2026-11-02, Admin API 2026-10, below) showed that this is wrong in two ways for customs,
which wants what the buyer actually paid:

1. **It is before discounts.** `originalUnitPriceSet` is the list price. A discount
   overstates the declared value, the duties the recipient pays, and the value checked
   against the EEI, IOSS and UK thresholds.
2. **It is in the shop's currency, not the buyer's.** On a USD store a Canadian buyer
   pays in CAD (the presentment currency). `shopMoney` is converted from that.

This matters only where PolyBag writes the customs declaration: a direct USPS, UPS or FedEx
Label for a Shopify order. A Label bought through Shopify Shipping is a blind purchase, and
Shopify fills in its own customs form.

## What Shopify reports

From three captures of two CAD orders on a USD store: one with a manual line discount and a
manual order discount; one with a fixed-amount order discount code and a fixed-amount
product discount code, on one line of quantity 3; and the second again after an item was
added by an order edit.

- **`presentmentMoney` is what the buyer paid,** and every line, discount and total agrees
  in it: the original line total, less the discounts allocated to the line, summed over
  the lines, is the order's total to the cent.
- **`shopMoney` is a conversion of the presentment amounts,** each amount rounded on its
  own. Two discount codes set at US$2 and US$1 report `shopMoney` allocations of 2.03 and
  1.02. The shop amounts are not the store's original figures and add nothing for customs.
- **`discountedUnitPriceSet` and `discountedTotalSet` miss discount codes.** A manual line
  discount appeared in them; neither discount code did, and the line's `totalDiscountSet`
  was zero. Do not use them, or `totalDiscountSet`.
- **`discountAllocations` holds every discount on the line,** including the line's share
  of an order-level discount, in both currencies.
- **`discountedUnitPriceAfterAllDiscountsSet` is rounded per unit.** On a line of
  CA$48.00 with CA$4.25 of discounts it reports 14.59, which times 3 is 43.77, not the
  43.75 charged.
- **The fulfillment order line carries no discounted price.** Its own
  `originalUnitPriceSet` is the list price. The nested `lineItem` has the fields above.
- **An order edit does not respread discounts.** Adding an item left the existing line's
  allocations unchanged and added the new line at full price, with no allocation; the
  totals still agreed. The fulfillment order kept its ID, the order's `updatedAt` moved,
  and `edited` became true. A line with a discount applied cannot have its quantity
  edited in the admin, so a reduced quantity on a discounted line was not observed.

## Desired behavior

- **Currency.** The Shipment's `currency` is the order's `presentmentCurrencyCode` (or the
  `presentmentMoney.currencyCode` on the lines), and every value is a `presentmentMoney`
  amount.
- **Line value after all discounts.** For each fulfillment order line, take the nested
  `lineItem`'s `originalTotalSet.presentmentMoney` less the sum of its
  `discountAllocations[].allocatedAmountSet.presentmentMoney`. That is the exact amount
  the buyer paid for the whole line item.
- **Partly fulfilled lines.** A fulfillment order may hold only part of a line's quantity
  (`remainingQuantity`), so the line value it declares is the line's value times
  `remainingQuantity / lineItem.quantity`.
- **Item value.** `shipment_items.value` is a unit value to the cent, so it is the line
  value divided by the quantity, rounded to the cent. A multi-unit line can then be a
  cent or two off what was charged, which is immaterial for customs.
- **Shipment value.** The sum of the exact line values, rounded once to the cent, not the
  sum of the rounded unit values times quantity.
- **A line given away** (discounted to zero, or to under half a cent a unit) keeps its
  list price. Goods have a customs value when nothing was paid for them, and a line at
  zero is refused before purchase (`CustomsReadiness::zeroValueLines()`).

## Notes

- The goods fingerprint (`ShopifyGoodsFingerprint`) does not include price, so this
  changes no fingerprints.
- Shipments already imported keep their list-price, shop-currency values until the order
  is read again: within a day for an open one, since the import reads every open
  fulfillment order daily and `value` is in the row's checksum. Each changes once. A manager's currency on a Shipment is preserved on re-import
  (`01`), so a Shipment a manager changed keeps its currency.
- The probe captures and the read-only script are in `.scratch/order-currency/`, which is
  gitignored.
- The Amazon import sums the `ITEM` proceeds breakdown, which should already be the amount
  after promotions (`ItemPromotionBreakdown` is a separate type). Confirm from a real
  discounted order rather than assume it. Not in this issue.

## Comments

**2026-11-02, probed.** Read-only captures of two test orders on a development store,
before and after an order edit. Findings above. Both of the issue's defaults hold: declare
after all discounts, in the presentment currency. The line value is computed from the
allocations because the after-all-discounts unit price is rounded per unit and the
"discounted" fields omit discount codes. A partial refund was not captured; the line's
`currentQuantity` and refunds do not change what was paid for the units still to ship,
so it is not expected to matter.

**2026-11-02, built.**

- **Line value.** `ShopifySource::paidUnitValue()` takes the line item's
  `originalUnitPriceSet.presentmentMoney` less its `discountAllocations`, spread over
  `lineItem.quantity`. A fulfillment order line's value is that times its
  `remainingQuantity`, so a fulfillment order holding part of a line declares its share.
  The list price times the quantity is the line's original total, so the query does not
  also read `originalTotalSet`.
- **Currency.** The Shipment's currency is the lines' `presentmentMoney.currencyCode`.
  `shopMoney` is no longer read for prices; the duties check still reads
  `originalTotalDutiesSet.shopMoney`, which only tests for a positive amount.
- **Query cost.** The added fields push the open fulfillment orders page past Shopify's
  limit: 250 a page requested 1,014 points, where it requested 937 before. Shopify's cost
  steps rather than scaling with the page: any page from 150 to 240 requests 922, and
  100 requests 830, all measured read-only on the development store with a part-number
  metafield selected. The page is now 200. By-ID reads went from 85 to 92 points per 250,
  and the rest of one fulfillment order's items from 113 to 124, both well under.
- **Not covered.** A partial refund was not captured, as above.

**2026-11-02, after review.**

- **Free lines.** The spec first said a line discounted to zero imports at zero "as it
  does today". That was wrong: the list price was declared before, and a zero line would
  have blocked every international direct label for an order with a free gift. A line
  given away now keeps its list price.
- **Later pages of line items.** The query for a fulfillment order's items past the first
  40 carries the same price fields, now covered by a test.
- **Not covered: tax-inclusive prices.** Both orders were CAD with no tax. A market priced
  with tax included (UK or EU buyers shown duty- and tax-inclusive prices) may report
  `originalUnitPriceSet` with VAT in it, which the customs value and the UK and IOSS
  thresholds exclude. `shopMoney` had the same exposure. Unprobed; opened as
  `tax-inclusive-prices/01`.
- **Not covered: the order-value shipping rule** still compares the bare `value`, now
  more often in a foreign currency. `01` left this for a decision, and it was kept as a
  raw number for now (2026-11-01).
