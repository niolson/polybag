# Ship page: say which order it is, make the rate rows readable, and show a failed purchase

> *This was generated by AI during the ui-review walk-through.*

Status: done

Source: [findings](../findings.md) S2, S3, S8; with S4, S5, S6, M4

Page or flow: Ship page (`/ship/{package}`), demo step 3

Severity: medium

Call: before the demo

## Problem

- **S3.** The heading is "Ship". The Shipment reference appears nowhere, there is no link back
  to Pack or the Shipment, and nothing is lit in the sidebar.
- **S2.** Rate rows read "$8.62 — MILITARY — LATE": USPS's raw commitment name, a computed
  flag, both in capitals, in small grey text; the price is the least prominent thing.
  `RateResponse::formDescription()` and `EloquentPackageShippingWorkflow` (" — LATE").
- **S8.** When USPS sold a label and PolyBag failed to record it, the page stayed as it was,
  Buy & Print still enabled, no error. The only trace was two identical bell notifications.
  After a reload the page did not say a sold label was waiting to be recovered.
- **S4–S6, M4.** "2 items" counts lines; carriers with no rate are not mentioned; the customs
  description warning names a SKU, not the text; a manual shipment shows an empty Items card.

## Fix

1. Heading or subheading with the reference and recipient; links to Pack and the Shipment; add
   the Ship route to Scan & Pack's active route pattern.
2. Rate rows: price in its own right-aligned column; delivery date, or a readable commitment
   ("Military mail, no date"); Late and Unknown (see `01`) as badges.
3. Show a recording failure inline with the notification's text, send the notification once,
   and on load say when a sold label is waiting and that Buy will recover it.
4. The low items as listed in the findings.

## Done when

Tests cover the heading, the badge rendering, and the inline failure state (fake adapter
that sells and then fails to record).

## Comments

### 2026-10-17 — done

- **S3.** The heading is "Ship {reference}", with a subheading naming the recipient, the
  place and the Package, and links Back to Pack and View Shipment (the second only for
  someone who may view it). Scan & Pack stays lit in the sidebar on the Ship page.
- **S2.** Each rate row has its price in a right-aligned column, then the delivery in
  words: the date, else the commitment or transit time in sentence case, with USPS's
  military commitment read as "Military mail, no date". Late and No delivery estimate are
  badges, shown only against a due-by date. `RateResponse::formDescription()` became
  `priceDescription()` and `deliveryDescription()`; the workflow puts the delivery state on
  each rate as `delivery`.
- **S8.** A refused purchase is shown on the page in a callout with the result's own text,
  as well as the toast, until the next attempt. On load, a Package with a label sold but not
  saved, or a purchase nobody heard back about, says so and offers **Check Earlier
  Purchase**, which calls `checkEarlierPurchases()` without needing a rate (none may come
  back, and Buy needs a selection): a label that comes back ships and prints, and "nothing
  bought" frees the Package with fresh rates. Where the seller cannot hand the label over
  again, it says not to buy another and sends a manager to the package page. A Shopify
  blind purchase keeps no offer, so its markers on the Package are read too: the notice says
  to buy through Shopify again, which picks that purchase up, or, with no Shopify option
  offered, not to buy another. The bell
  notification is sent once per Package and label: a user who already has it unread is not
  sent it again.
- **S4.** Items counts units, and says "2 lines, 3 items" when the two differ.
- **S5.** A short line names the sellers that were asked and returned no rate, from
  `ShippingRateService::getUnratedSources()`. It says only that no rate came back: the
  carriers give no reason PolyBag could show.
- **S6.** The USPS description warning is titled "Customs Description Will Be Shortened",
  quotes each description and its 30-character cut, says the text is the product's
  description or name, and links to the product.
- **M4.** A Package with no items says "No items: manual shipment." in the Items card.
