# Key direct integrations by `carriers.adapter`

Status: needs-triage

Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md), *Catalog* and *Constraints from the app*. ADR-0006 decision 1.

## What to build

Whether a carrier has a direct integration becomes a fact on its row, not a match on
its name. Renaming a carrier stops breaking rating, buying, manifests and the reference
data. A carrier account can be created only for a carrier we actually buy from.

- **The column.** `carriers.adapter` is a nullable, unique key: `usps`, `ups` or `fedex`
  here, and `amazon_shipping` from `15`. `CarrierSeeder` sets it on the three direct
  carriers. It is null on every other row, including the `Shopify` and `Amazon` rows,
  which remain until `09` and `12`. The carrier form shows it read-only and never lets an
  operator edit it.
- **The seeders find carriers by the key.** `CarrierSeeder` runs on every container
  start, through `app:sync-reference-data` in `docker/entrypoint.sh`. It calls
  `firstOrCreate(['name' => 'UPS'])` today, so renaming UPS and restarting creates a
  second `UPS` carrier. That bug predates this work.
  - The direct carriers are found by `adapter` and created with their name only when no
    row carries the key.
  - `CarrierAliasSeeder` finds its carriers the same way. It skips a renamed carrier's
    aliases today.
  - Carriers with no adapter are found by name. The name is all they have.
- **The registry.**
  - `CarrierRegistry` finds a direct adapter by the key, never by a carrier's name.
  - Shopify and Amazon keep their registrations as sources, and the two fake rows keep
    reaching them by name until `09` and `12` remove the rows. Name that as the only
    remaining name lookup, and mark it transitional in the code.
  - Test helpers register fakes by key. Eighteen test files call `registerInstance()`
    by carrier name today.
- **Every lookup by name moves to the key**, or to `normalized_carrier_id` where it
  reads a package. There are about twenty call sites. The ones on a purchase path are
  the reason a rename breaks more than rating:
  - rating: `ShippingRateService`'s task runner, `sellersForShippingMethod()`, and the
    no-method fallback's `getActiveCarrierServicesForCarrierName()`;
  - account resolution: `ResolvesCarrierAccount::resolveAccount()` and its second lookup,
    and `EloquentPackageShippingWorkflow::accountNoLongerResolves()`. The last refuses
    every direct purchase as "Carrier Account Changed" once its carrier is renamed;
  - dispatch: `CarrierAccountPostageSource`, which resolves void, tracking and manifests
    from `packages.carrier` today, and `PostageSourceDispatcher`;
  - End of Day and manifests: `EndOfDay` counts and manifests packages by
    `where('carrier', $carrier->name)`, and `ManifestService` finds USPS by name. Both
    read `normalized_carrier_id` and the key instead;
  - `UspsAddressValidator` (USPS by name), `SpecialServiceResolver::resolveForPackageAndRate()`
    (carrier name plus code, which reads `RateResponse::$carrierServiceId` from `02`
    instead), `FedexSandboxInternationalRates`, the Setup Wizard, and the three FedEx
    test-case commands.

  A package bought directly has a `carrier_account_id`, so its account's carrier is the
  answer. Nothing needs to parse `packages.carrier`, which stays free text for the
  carrier of record.
- **Carrier accounts.** The Carrier Account form offers only carriers whose adapter takes
  a `CarrierAccount`. `CarrierAccount` refuses to save against any other carrier. That
  replaces the form's name check against `Amazon`, and closes the gap that let an account
  be created for `Shopify`. Amazon Shipping's adapter (`15`) takes a connection instead,
  so the adapter declares which kind of account it uses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After UPS is renamed in the carrier form, UPS still rates, buys (the purchase's
      account check included), voids, tracks and manifests, which is shown by a test
- [ ] After USPS is renamed, address validation and the SCAN form still work
- [ ] Renaming a direct carrier and running `app:sync-reference-data` creates no second
      row, and its aliases are still seeded
- [ ] Two carriers cannot share an `adapter` value
- [ ] The Carrier Account form lists only USPS, UPS and FedEx, and saving an account for
      any other carrier is refused, `Shopify` included
- [ ] No lookup of a direct carrier by name remains, and the two transitional source
      lookups are marked as such
- [ ] The existing suite passes
