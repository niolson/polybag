# A connection's postage setting replaces the client blind-purchase opt-in

Status: needs-triage

Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md), *Connections*. ADR-0006 decision 6.

## What to build

Consent to buy channel postage on a client's own Shopify or Amazon account lives on that
account's connection. It only narrows: it never makes anything buyable that the shipping
method, or until `13` the approvals, would not.

- **The setting.** Shopify and Amazon connections gain a postage setting:
  - *does not sell postage*
  - *packer only*
  - *packer and automation*

  The default is *does not sell postage* for Shopify, today's opt-in default, and
  *packer only* for Amazon. It sits on the connection form, which is Admin-only.
- **What it covers.** Shopify's blind purchase, and Amazon Buy Shipping for the
  connection's own Amazon orders.
- **What it does not cover.** Amazon Shipping sold to orders from other channels.
  `offers_off_amazon_shipping` and the scope still decide whether a connection sells
  that at all. Until `15` it stays under the approvals for orders from other channels.
  From `15` it is a direct sale, bought like UPS whenever the method lists the service
  (ADR-0006 decision 6). Keep the two switches visibly separate on the form.
- **What each value does.**
  - *Does not sell postage*: the source is not asked.
  - *Packer only*: its offers appear on the Ship page. Automation refuses them, naming
    the connection's setting as the reason, and a rule or the sole-choice rule cannot
    reach them.
  - *Packer and automation*: automation may buy them, subject to everything else.
- **Remove `clients.blind_purchase_enabled`.** That covers the column, the client form
  toggle, the single-client toggle in Settings, the checks in `ShopifyAdapter` and
  `EloquentPackageShippingWorkflow`, and the reference in `ServiceApprovalPolicy`'s
  docblock.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Shopify connection set to *does not sell postage* produces no blind offers
- [ ] Set to *packer only*, its offers show but no rule, batch or sole choice buys one
- [ ] Set to *packer and automation*, automation behaves as the opt-in did
- [ ] An Amazon connection set to *packer only* has its Amazon orders' offers shown and
      refused unattended, with the setting named, even when approved
- [ ] Set to *packer and automation*, approvals still decide until `13`
- [ ] The postage setting does not change what a connection sells to orders from other
      channels
- [ ] `clients.blind_purchase_enabled` no longer exists
