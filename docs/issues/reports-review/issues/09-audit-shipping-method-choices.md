# Investigate auditing shipping method choices

Status: needs-triage
Category: investigation

> *This was generated by AI during the reports review, from the maintainer's description of
> what Rate Comparison was meant to show.*

## Question

Which packages could a cheaper shipping method have delivered by the date they had to
arrive? A parcel to an address in the same state with a two-day window usually makes
Ground just as well as a two-day service, and the two-day service only costs more.

Rate Comparison (removed in [`01`](01-remove-rate-comparison-and-packing-validation.md))
couldn't answer it. It compared against the cheapest quote in the session, whatever its
delivery date, and a session only holds the services of the method the shipment came in
with. These are often trade-offs rather than mistakes, so the answer should be "this
alternative would have arrived on day N for $X less", not a pass/fail.

## What exists (at `211d3864`)

- Rating asks each source for the method's services only, but the USPS, UPS and FedEx
  adapters receive every service the carrier returns and drop the rest after parsing
  (`UspsAdapter::isValidRate()`, `UpsAdapter::extractRateDetails()`,
  `FedexAdapter::extractRateDetails()`). Those dropped rates, with their delivery
  estimates where the carrier gives one, are already in hand at no extra API cost.
- `rate_quotes` logs only kept rates, and `quoted_delivery_date` is mostly empty: 148 of
  10,007 locally. Whether that's the carriers or the adapters not mapping the field needs
  checking per carrier.
- A carrier with no service in the method isn't asked at all, so its rates are never seen.

## Options to investigate

1. **Log what the adapters drop**, flagged as outside the method, with the delivery date
   mapped for every carrier that returns one. Cheapest; covers carriers already on the
   method.
2. **Shadow rating**: a queued job, after purchase, that rates the package against every
   active service on the eligible accounts, apart from the purchase path, so it can never
   slow or affect a purchase. Covers every carrier, costs API calls, and has to respect each
   carrier's rate limits and terms.
3. **The report**: per package, the cheapest alternative whose estimated delivery is no
   later than `deliver_by` (or the bought service's estimate), and the saving. Rolled up by
   shipping method and destination zone, it suggests where a method's services could change.

## To settle

  estimates are against `delivered_at`.
- Whether option 1 alone is enough to be worth a report.
- Retention: dropped rates would multiply the size of `rate_quotes`.
