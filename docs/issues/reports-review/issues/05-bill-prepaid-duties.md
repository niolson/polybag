# Bill prepaid duties in Client Billing

Status: ready-for-agent

> *This was generated by AI during the reports review.*

## Problem

A 3PL prepays duties, taxes and fees on DDP Labels (`package_labels.duties_cost`), and
Client Billing never bills them (`release-polish/33`, product decision).

Decided 2026-11-24: bill them.

## Fix

- A **Duties** column in the summary, the billable event log and both CSVs, summing the
  active Labels' `duties_cost` on the period's packages, included in the line total.
- A DDP Label whose duties are not recorded (UPS and FedEx bill after clearance) is flagged
  on its line, the way unpriced postage is, rather than billed as $0.00 silently.

## Done when

Tests for a USPS DDP label billed, a voided one not billed, and an unrecorded one flagged.
