# Delivery Performance report

Status: done

> *This was generated by AI during the reports review.*

## Problem

Tracking fills `packages.delivered_at` and `tracking_status`, and nothing reports how each
carrier and service performs once a parcel has left.

## Fix

A report over Packages shipped in the range:

- Group by Carrier, Carrier + Service, or Destination (domestic / international).
- Columns: Packages, Delivered, Delivered %, Median and 90th-percentile transit days
  (`shipped_at` to `delivered_at`), With a Deliver-By, On Time (delivered on or before
  `deliver_by` in the Location's timezone), On-Time %, In Exception, Stuck Pre-Transit
  (pre-transit more than 48 hours after shipping, as on the dashboard).
- **Export CSV**.

## Done when

Tests for transit times, on-time against `deliver_by`, and the exception and pre-transit
counts.

## Comments

- 2026-11-25 — Done as `DeliveryPerformanceReport`, after Fulfillment Speed. Group by Carrier (default), Service (the active Label's catalog service after the carrier's display name), Destination (domestic when the Shipment's country is the origin Location's) or Week Shipped; Location and Client filters where enabled. On time is the delivery's local day on or before `deliver_by`. Late is delivered after it, or still out once it passed. A package with its deliver-by still ahead is not judged. Exceptions and stuck pre-transit match `ExceptionsWidget`; Returned is its own column. The nearest-rank percentile moved to `App\Filament\Support\Percentile`, which Fulfillment Speed now uses too.
