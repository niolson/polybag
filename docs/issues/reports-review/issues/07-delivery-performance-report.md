# Delivery Performance report

Status: ready-for-agent

> *This was generated by AI during the reports review.*

## Problem

Tracking fills `packages.delivered_at` and `tracking_status`, and nothing reports how each
carrier and service performs once a parcel has left.

## Fix

A report over Packages shipped in the range:

- Group by Carrier, Carrier + Service, or Destination (domestic / international).
- Columns: Packages, Delivered, Delivered %, Median and 90th-percentile transit days
  (`shipped_at` to `delivered_at`), With a Deliver-By, On Time (delivered on or before
  `deliver_by` in the Location's timezone), On-Time %, In Exception, Stuck Pre-Transit
  (pre-transit more than 48 hours after shipping, as on the dashboard).
- **Export CSV**.

## Done when

Tests for transit times, on-time against `deliver_by`, and the exception and pre-transit
counts.
