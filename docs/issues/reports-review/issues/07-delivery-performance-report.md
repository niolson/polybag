# Delivery Performance report

Status: done

> *This was generated by AI during the reports review.*

## Problem

Tracking fills `packages.delivered_at` and `tracking_status`, and nothing reports how each
carrier and service performs once a parcel has left.

## Fix

A report over Packages shipped in the range:

- Group by Carrier, Carrier + Service, or Destination (domestic / international).
- Columns: Packages, Delivered, Delivered %, Median and 90th-percentile transit days
  (`shipped_at` to `delivered_at`), With a Deliver-By, On Time (delivered on or before
  `deliver_by` in the Location's timezone), On-Time %, In Exception, Stuck Pre-Transit
  (pre-transit more than 48 hours after shipping, as on the dashboard).
- **Export CSV**.

## Done when

Tests for transit times, on-time against `deliver_by`, and the exception and pre-transit
counts.

## Comments

- 2026-11-25 — Done as `DeliveryPerformanceReport`, after Fulfillment Speed. Group by Carrier (default), Service (the active Label's catalog service after the carrier's display name), Destination (domestic when the Shipment's country is the origin Location's) or Week Shipped; Location and Client filters where enabled. On time is the delivery's local day on or before `deliver_by`. Late is delivered after it, or still out once it passed. A package with its deliver-by still ahead is not judged. Exceptions and stuck pre-transit match `ExceptionsWidget`; Returned is its own column. The nearest-rank percentile moved to `App\Filament\Support\Percentile`, which Fulfillment Speed now uses too. Same one-row-per-package read as `06`: 0.25 s for about 2,600 packages locally on MySQL. Tests in `DeliveryPerformanceReportTest` and `tests/Unit/PercentileTest.php`.
- 2026-11-25 — Review fixes. A package is delivered when its tracking status says Delivered, as `ValidationQualityReport` already treats it, not only when `delivered_at` is set: Shopify and Amazon can report Delivered without a time, and USPS gives none it cannot vouch for. Such a package has no transit time and no on-time verdict, rather than being counted undelivered and then late. Delivered % is over packages with any tracking status; the rest show as No Tracking. Locally that moves USPS from 83% to 99%. A package with no Location is judged against the default Location's country. The report needs a From date. Per-package judging of multi-box orders and the default Location's calendar for delivery days are now in the class docblock.
