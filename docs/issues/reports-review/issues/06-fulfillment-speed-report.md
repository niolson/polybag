# Fulfillment Speed report

Status: done

> *This was generated by AI during the reports review.*

## Problem

`ship_by` is imported from Amazon, Shopify and Database sources, and nothing reports
whether orders left by it, or how long orders wait before they ship.

## Fix

A report over Shipments whose first package shipped in the range:

- Group by Day, Week, Month, Channel or Client (multi-client).
- Columns: Orders Shipped, Median and 90th-percentile hours from import to first package
  shipped, Orders With a Ship-By, Shipped On Time (first package's local ship date on or
  before `ship_by`), On-Time %, Late.
- A headline of open orders already past their ship-by.
- **Export CSV**.

The clock starts at `shipments.created_at`, the import time, not when the order was placed;
the page says so.

## Done when

Tests for on-time and late in the Location's timezone, orders with no ship-by left out of
the rate, and each grouping.

## Comments

- 2026-11-25 — Done as `FulfillmentSpeedReport`, between Volume and Packages by User. Group by Day, Week (default), Month, Channel or Client (multi-client). Manual Ship orders (no `data_source_id`) and drafts are left out, since they are created at the bench. An order whose first package shipped before the range is left out of it. Headline cards: orders shipped, median time to ship, shipped-by-ship-by rate with the late count, and open orders past ship-by now, linking to the Shipments list's `ship_by_due=overdue` filter. Medians and 90th percentiles are nearest-rank in PHP over one row per order, through Filament's custom-data `records()` table. Locally that is 0.28 s for about 2,000 orders on MySQL. A very large range on a busy install holds every order in range in memory; if that bites, pre-aggregate per day. Tests in `FulfillmentSpeedReportTest`.
