# Count a Shopify `auto` label when its carrier's day was never ended

Status: needs-triage
Category: bug
Created: 2026-10-02

Repo: `polybag`

## Parent

Left open by [`carrier-catalog-reset/08`](../../archive/carrier-catalog-reset/issues/08-date-by-expected-carrier-and-end-of-day.md),
whose last review comment names the gap. ADR-0006 decision 9: a label is dated by the
carrier expected to carry it, and End of Day ends one day per carrier across every source.

## Problem

End of Day counts a carrier's labels by carrier of record (`normalized_carrier_id`) and
lets a label in by either of two tests (`EndOfDay::loadData()`):

1. its `ship_date` is the carrier's current ship date, or
2. it was bought after an End of Day that ended the carrier's current batch
   (`ShipDateService::lastEndOfDayForCurrentBatch()`).

A Shopify `auto` purchase is dated *before* Shopify picks the carrier, by the connection's
*Date Shopify's choice as* carrier (USPS by default). When Shopify then puts it on another
carrier, the label is counted under that carrier, but only if one of the two tests passes.

If nobody pressed End Shipping Day for that carrier, test 2 has no End of Day to measure
from and returns null. Test 1 fails whenever the carrier's current date differs from the
date the label was given. Then the label is counted on no row at all.

Example: UPS's cutoff is 4 PM and USPS's is 8 PM. At 5 PM Wednesday a Shopify `auto` label
is dated Wednesday by USPS, and Shopify puts it on UPS. UPS's current ship date is already
Thursday. Nobody ended UPS's day, so neither test passes, and the parcel the UPS driver
takes is missing from the UPS row. Different pickup days on the two carriers cause the
same miss.

Only Shopify `auto` labels are affected. Direct rates and every Amazon offer are dated by
the carrier the offer names, so their dates always come from their carrier of record.

## Questions

- Should the current batch have a start when no End of Day ended it, for example the
  previous pickup day's cutoff from `ShipDateService`? Test 2 could then measure from that
  instead of returning null, and the count would stop depending on someone pressing End
  Shipping Day.
- Or should test 1 accept any date from the carrier's previous pickup day to its current
  one, for labels whose date came from another carrier's policy?
- Either change must not bring back what the 2026-09-25 reviews of `08` removed: labels
  from weeks ago counted under a stale End of Day, and weekend or after-cutoff labels
  missed when a day had never been ended.
- Is a mismatch rare enough in practice (USPS default, few Shopify `auto` labels that land
  on UPS or FedEx) that a note on the page is enough?

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Shopify `auto` label dated by USPS and carried by UPS is counted on the UPS row
      when UPS's day was never ended and UPS's current date differs from the label's
- [ ] The cases covered by `ShipDateByExpectedCarrierTest` and the End of Day tests still
      pass: no stale End of Day counts old labels, and weekend and after-cutoff labels
      still count
- [ ] The manifest count is unchanged: it stays by ship date and direct labels only
