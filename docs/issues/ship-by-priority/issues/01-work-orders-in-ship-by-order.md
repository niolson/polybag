# Work orders in ship-by order

Status: needs-triage
Category: enhancement
Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Problem

`ship_by` is the date an order must leave the warehouse. Amazon measures a seller's late
shipment rate against it, and Shopify's `fulfillBy` means the same thing. PolyBag imports
it and shows it, but no part of the workflow uses it to decide what happens next.

What exists today (commit `a872e763`):

- **Import:** Amazon reads `fulfillment.shipByWindow.latestDateTime`, Shopify reads
  `fulfillBy`, and the Database driver can map `ship_by`. The Amazon windows are also kept
  in `metadata.amazon_ship_by_window`.
- **Shipments list:** a Ship By column, red when overdue, and a filter for due today,
  past due, or both, using `Shipment::scopeDueToShip()` and `scopeShipByToday()` in the
  Location's timezone. Batch Ship runs from this list, so an operator can already filter
  to the orders due today and ship them.

What does not use it:

- **Pick batches** are ordered by `shipping_methods.is_expedited`, then `created_at`
  (`PickBatchService`). An order due today can wait behind newer, non-expedited orders
  with later ship-by dates, and an "expedited" method is only a stand-in for urgency the
  order already states.
- **The Pack page header** shows the reference, the recipient's name and city. It doesn't
  show the ship-by date or warn when it has passed (`ui-review` finding P6).
- **The dashboard** has no count of open orders due to ship today or past their ship-by
  date. `ExceptionsWidget` counts other exceptions but not this one.

Rate selection is deliberately left alone. Ship-by is about when the parcel leaves, and
the service is chosen against `deliver_by` or the shipping method's delivery commitment.

## Proposed decisions

Each one needs a maintainer to confirm it:

- **Pick batches order by ship-by first.** The order is: `ship_by` ascending with nulls
  last, then the existing expedited flag when that option is on, then `created_at`. An
  order with no ship-by date keeps its current place behind dated ones. Should this be a
  new toggle on Generate Pick Batch, or should it replace the order outright? Replacing it
  is simpler, and nothing dated would be picked later than it is today.
- **The Pack page shows the ship-by date** in the shipment header: "Ship by Tue, Nov 24",
  shown as a warning on the day itself and as danger once it has passed. It shows only
  when the Shipment has a ship-by date.
- **`ExceptionsWidget` counts open Shipments past their ship-by date** and links to the
  Shipments list with the "Past ship-by date" filter. It shows only when the count is
  non-zero. Draft Shipments are excluded, as they are everywhere else.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A generated pick batch takes open, pending Shipments in ship-by order, earliest
      first, with undated ones after dated ones
- [ ] With "prioritize expedited" on, ship-by still comes first and expedited breaks ties
- [ ] The Pack page header shows the ship-by date, styled by whether it is due today or
      past, and shows nothing when there is none
- [ ] `ExceptionsWidget` shows the past-ship-by count and links to the filtered list, and
      counts the same Shipments as the list's "Past ship-by date" filter
- [ ] "Today" is the Location's calendar day in all three places, as in the existing scopes

## Out of scope

- Using ship-by in rate selection or automation. A service is chosen by `deliver_by` or
  the method's delivery commitment
- Holding or blocking a Shipment past its ship-by date. It can still be shipped, and
  Amazon still wants it shipped
- Flagging Amazon orders long past ship-by as missed cancellations:
  [`pii-retention/04`](../../pii-retention/issues/04-find-old-open-shipments.md)
- The Pack page header's other gaps from P6 (shipping method, channel, a link to the
  Shipment), unless they come along for free
