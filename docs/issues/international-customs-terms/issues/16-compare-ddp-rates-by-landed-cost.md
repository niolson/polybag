# International rate shopping compares transport prices; a DDP parcel's duties and taxes are not in the comparison

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md), *Later*: "Duty and tax quotes at rate time". `08` added
`package_labels.duties_package_fee`, `duties_duty` and `duties_tax` beside `duties_cost`
(2026-10-15), so the actual prepaid charge has somewhere to live; this is the estimate that
precedes it. Raised by the owner on 2026-10-15.

## Problem

Rates are ranked on the transport price alone. For a DDU parcel that is the whole cost to
the shipper. For a DDP parcel it is not: the shipper also pays duties, taxes and a carrier or
provider fee, and those differ by carrier.

- **USPS** prepays them at label purchase (`duties_cost`, a package fee plus duty and tax per
  line). A DDP label to Germany bought at $79.61 postage carried $286.06 of duties and taxes
  on TEM (`08`, package 243).
- **UPS and FedEx** bill them to the account after clearance, with brokerage fees, so they are
  not known at purchase at all.

So a DDP parcel can look cheapest on the carrier that quotes the lowest postage and cost the
most once duties are counted, or the reverse. Nothing on the Ship page, in batch ship, or in
automation's choice of rate says so.

## Desired behavior

**A quote, kept apart from the price.** A DDP rate carries an optional estimate of duties,
taxes and fees, with its parts, its source and the time it was quoted. It is never added to
`RateResponse::$price`, and never stored in `duties_cost`, which stays the carrier's reported
actual. The postage price remains the only amount an Offer is bound to.

**Where the estimate comes from** (all three need checking against the carrier's own
reference before building; none has been called):

| Carrier | Source named in the PRD | Known constraint |
|---|---|---|
| USPS | `total-rates/search` with `requestDutiesTaxesFees`, which returns a `dutiesTaxesFeesQuote` and extra service 371 | The 2026-01-28 release notes: the quote comes back with the rate option |
| FedEx | Estimated duties and taxes (EDT) | Needs real commodities in the rate request; today's rate request uses a $1 placeholder |
| UPS | Landed Cost | Needs the line items, origin, HS codes and values |

Whether each quote matches what the carrier later bills is itself unknown. USPS's DDP fee,
the provider's duty and tax figures, and TEM test data all differ from production, so the
comparison is labelled as an estimate everywhere it appears.

**Ship page.** A DDP rate shows "Est. duties and taxes" and an estimated landed total beside
its postage, both marked as estimates. A rate with no estimate says "Duties not estimated"
rather than showing zero. A DDU rate is unchanged.

**Ranking.** Rates rank by landed cost only when the resolved term is DDP and every
candidate has an estimate. Otherwise the order stays as it is today and the page says the
comparison is postage only. Automation and batch ship do not change their choice on an
estimate alone until someone decides they should: decide this before building, since it
changes what an unattended purchase picks.

**Afterwards.** The estimate chosen is snapshotted on the Label (for example
`estimated_duties_cost`) so the DDP report can show estimate against actual. For UPS and
FedEx the actual is entered later, from the invoice; that is a separate piece of work.

## Questions to settle first

- Does the estimate change which rate automation picks, or only what a person sees?
- Does a failed or slow quote hide the rate, show it without an estimate, or block it?
  Recommendation: show it without an estimate and say so.
- FedEx's rate request needs commodities. Is that in scope here, or does FedEx get no
  estimate until it is done?
- Quotes cost an extra call per carrier per rate request. Cache them against the same quote
  inputs the Offer fingerprint uses, so a change to a line retires the estimate with the Offer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A DDP rate from each carrier that can estimate carries duties, taxes and fees, kept
      apart from its price; a DDU rate carries none
- [ ] The Offer's price and its fingerprint are unchanged by an estimate
- [ ] The Ship page marks every estimate as one and never shows a missing estimate as zero
- [ ] With every DDP candidate estimated, the page ranks by landed cost and says so; with any
      missing, it ranks as today and says the comparison is postage only
- [ ] Automation and batch ship pick the same rate as before, until the question above is
      answered in this issue
- [ ] The chosen estimate is stored on the Label and never in `duties_cost`
- [ ] A failed duty quote never fails the rate request
- [ ] Tests for each carrier use Saloon fakes; no test calls a carrier
