# Resolve customs terms per Shipment and filter rates by carrier duties support

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement
Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md), *Resolution*; ADR-0008 decisions 1–4. Needs `03`.

## Problem

Once the data exists, something has to turn the order, the client and the destination
into one answer before rating, and the rate list has to respect what each carrier can do
with it.

## Desired behavior

**Resolver.** A `CustomsTermsResolver` returns a `ResolvedCustomsTerms` DTO for a
Shipment and destination: the duties term (or *unresolved*), where it came from (`order`,
`client`), the seller registration and where it came from, or none, and whether the
order value is over that regime's threshold (in which case no registration is sent).
Precedence as the PRD's *Resolution* block. An order registration whose regime does not
cover the destination is ignored. Domestic and same-customs-zone labels resolve to
nothing.

**Carrier support data.** `resources/data/customs/duties-support.json`, following
`resources/data/service-inference/`: a `version`, `notes`, and per carrier a map of
country → `{ support, source, checked, effective_from? }`, plus a per-carrier default for
unlisted countries. Seed USPS from the PRD's IMM table (LU, unlisted: `ddu_only`); UPS and
FedEx default to `either`. An entry with `effective_from` in the future is ignored until
then. A test fails on an unknown carrier, a non-ISO country, a bad `support` value or an
entry with no source.

**Rate filter.** After quoting, drop a rate whose carrier support does not fit the
resolved term (`ddp_required` with DDU, `ddu_only` with DDP). `08` adds a second reason
to the same filter, for a USPS account that has not accepted the DDP terms. Each dropped rate carries a reason the Ship
page shows: "USPS dropped: Germany requires prepaid duties (IMM)". When every rate is
dropped, the Ship page says why rather than showing an empty list.

**Offer fingerprint.** The resolved terms (term and registration) join the rate request
fingerprint, so editing the Shipment's `duties_terms` invalidates its Offers.

**Rate requests.** `RateRequest` carries the resolved terms, for `06`–`08` to send.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Resolver: order term beats client; most specific client entry beats `EU`; non-EU
      unset is DDU; EU unset is unresolved; order registration replaces client
      registration; a registration over its threshold is not sent; a mismatched order
      regime is ignored
- [ ] `duties-support.json` exists with sources for every USPS entry, and its test passes
- [ ] A DDU Shipment to DE gets no USPS rates, with the reason shown; a DDP Shipment to PL
      gets no USPS rates; a DDP Shipment to NL keeps them
- [ ] An `effective_from` entry in the future does not apply; one in the past does
- [ ] Changing `duties_terms` after quoting makes the old Offer unredeemable
