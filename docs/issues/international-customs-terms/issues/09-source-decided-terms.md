# Amazon Buy Shipping and Shopify Shipping offers have source-decided terms

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement
Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md); ADR-0008 decision 5. Needs `04`. Related to
`amazon-buy-shipping/09` (an international purchase, still blocked on a foreign order).

## Problem

PolyBag cannot set the duties terms on a label bought through Amazon or Shopify, and does
not yet know what either declares. Without a rule, `04`'s filter would treat them as
DDU-or-DDP guesses, and automation could buy them into the EU on terms nobody knows.

## Desired behaviour

Offers and blind purchases from these sources skip `04`'s filter and the unresolved-EU
refusal. The Ship page labels them "Duties: decided by Amazon" or "Duties: decided by
Shopify". The Label snapshot records `terms: source_decided` and the source.

**Automation withholds them for EU, GB, NO and AU destinations, on every unattended
path.** There are two, and both need the guard:

- **Quoted offers** (Amazon Buy Shipping): `RateSelector::selectForAutomation()` withholds
  them, naming the reason, as it withholds an unapproved Amazon service.
- **Blind purchases** (Shopify Shipping) never reach `RateSelector`. They are chosen in
  `EloquentPackageShippingWorkflow::selectedRateForAutoShip()`, either through a rule's
  pre-selected blind purchase or through
  `ShippingRateService::soleBlindPurchaseOfferForAutomation()` when it is the method's
  sole eligible choice. Both branches must hold the offer for a restricted destination,
  the same way they hold one the connection's postage setting refuses, but with its own
  reason. `UnattendedRateSelection::blindOffersHeldByPostageSetting` names the postage
  setting, so a destination hold needs a separate reason instead of being reported as a
  postage-setting refusal.

Every `autoShip()` caller — batch ship (`GenerateLabelJob`), Pack, Manual Ship, View
Package — goes through `selectedRateForAutoShip()`, so guarding it covers them all.

When a test purchase establishes what a source declares, it gets entries in
`duties-support.json` and is filtered like a carrier; this issue does not need that to
close.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An Amazon offer into the EU is listed for a client with no EU terms, and labelled
- [ ] Batch ship and auto-ship do not buy an Amazon offer into the EU, and say why; a
      domestic Amazon offer is unaffected
- [ ] A Shopify blind purchase into the EU, pre-selected by a shipping rule, is held by
      auto-ship and batch ship, with the destination named as the reason, not the
      postage setting
- [ ] A Shopify blind purchase into the EU that is the method's sole eligible choice is
      held the same way
- [ ] The same Shopify purchase to a domestic destination is still bought on both paths
- [ ] A person can still buy the Shopify purchase into the EU from the Ship page
- [ ] The Label snapshot records `source_decided` for both sources
