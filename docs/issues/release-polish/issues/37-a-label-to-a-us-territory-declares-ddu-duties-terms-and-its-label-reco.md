# A label to a US territory declares DDU duties terms, and its Label records them

> *This was generated by AI during release-polish pass 4.*

Status: needs-triage

Pass: 4 — bug (review of customs terms in carrier requests)

Page or flow: Ship page and batch ship, UPS and FedEx, destination Puerto Rico, Guam, the US Virgin Islands or another territory sent as country `US`

Severity: low — off the demo path; the term is DDU, which bills nobody new, but the record and the invoice state terms for a parcel that has no import duties to assign.

Verified: plausible. Line numbers are at `12592f33`; this area is unchanged on `dfd842c5`.

## Problem

`CustomsTermsResolver::crossesNoCustomsBorder()`
(`app/Services/Customs/CustomsTermsResolver.php:296`) asks
`AddressData::sharesCustomsZoneWith()`, which puts every territory in its own zone
(`app/DataTransferObjects/Shipping/AddressData.php:230`). A mainland-to-PR label therefore
resolves `applies: true`, and `dutiesTerms()` falls through to `DutiesTerms::Ddu` with origin
`default` (line 230), since the destination country is `US` and not in the EU. The adapters
then put those terms on the wire:

- UPS: the rate request gains `PaymentDetails` and `Shipper.ShipperNumber`
  (`UpsAdapter::ratesDutiesTerm()`, line 1731), the invoice gains `TermsOfShipment: DDU`
  (`buildTermsOfShipment()`, line 1689), and a recipient tax ID on the Shipment would go in
  `GlobalTaxInformation` (`sendsGlobalTaxInformation()`, line 1845).
- FedEx: `commercialInvoice.termsOfSale: DDU` and `dutiesPayment.paymentType: RECIPIENT`
  (`FedexAdapter::buildCustomsClearanceDetail()`, line 1747), and a recipient tax ID in
  recipient `tins`.
- The snapshot (`CustomsTermsSnapshot::forRequest()`) records `duties_terms: ddu`,
  `duties_terms_source: default` on the Label.

USPS is unaffected: it sends a territory label to the domestic endpoint
(`UspsAdapter::createShipment()`, line 573, and `declaresCustomsTerms()`, line 1370, both
test the country code).

The ITN is a separate case and should stay: EEI is required for mainland-to-PR and -USVI
shipments over $2,500 (15 CFR 30.2), so the EEI fields are correct there.

## Evidence

Scenario: a Client with no duties policy, a Shipment to `San Juan, PR` stored
as country `US`, state `PR`, a UPS rate. `RateRequest::fromPackage()` resolves
`applies: true, dutiesTerms: ddu, origin: default`; the UPS rate body carries
`PaymentDetails.ShipmentCharge[Type 01]`; the label body carries
`InternationalForms.TermsOfShipment: "DDU"`; the Label's `customs_terms` is
`{"duties_terms":"ddu","duties_terms_source":"default",…}`. FedEx to the same address sends
`termsOfSale: DDU` / `RECIPIENT`. Before #320, FedEx sent `SENDER` to territories, so this
lane's duties payer has already changed once without a decision about territories.

## What to build

Product decision first: whether a US territory is a customs crossing for
duties terms. If not, have `crossesNoCustomsBorder()` treat a territory or military
destination from the fifty states as not applicable (terms, registration, recipient tax ID),
while the adapters keep sending the customs declaration and the ITN they need. Test UPS and
FedEx bodies and the snapshot for a PR label.
