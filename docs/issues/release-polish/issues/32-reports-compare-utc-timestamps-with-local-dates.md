# Client Billing and the other reports compare UTC timestamps with local calendar dates

Page or flow: Client Billing, Rate Comparison, Shipments by User, Packing Validation

Severity: medium — Month-end labels bill in the wrong period, and dates display a day late in US evenings.

Verified: plausible. Line numbers are at `12592f33`; this area is unchanged on `dfd842c5`.

## Problem

The filters compare `shipped_at`, stored in UTC, with bare local dates:
`>= 'Y-m-d'` and `<= 'Y-m-d 23:59:59'` (`ClientBillingReport.php:176-178`,
`UserShipmentsReport.php:111-112`). The Date columns show the UTC date
(`->dateTime('M j, Y')`). A label bought at 18:00 PDT on September 30 is
`2026-10-01 01:00` UTC, so it bills in October. Rate Comparison and Packing Validation
also drop the whole "until" day, because `shipped_at <= 'Y-m-d'` ends at midnight
(`RateComparison.php:109`, `PackingValidationReport.php:82,149,211`). The Volume Report
and the dashboard avoid this, because they key on `ship_date`, a local date.

## What to build

Convert the from/until bounds from the default Location's timezone to
UTC before comparing, and give the Date columns that timezone. Treat "until" as the end
of that local day everywhere.

## Comments

- 2026-11-18 — Done. A new `App\Filament\Support\LocalDateRange` reads a report's
  from/until dates as the default Location's calendar days (`Location::timezone()`, which
  the `ship_by` filters also use). It turns them into UTC bounds, from the start of "from"
  up to, but not including, the start of the day after "until", so the whole "until" day is
  in the range and daylight saving time is handled. Client Billing (its period condition,
  so the summary, the detail and both CSVs), Rate Comparison, Shipments by User and all
  three Packing Validation sections now filter through it. Their Date columns and the
  billing detail CSV show the Location's time. The default periods and the date-range
  subheading use the Location's today, as does the Volume Report's "in progress" period,
  so on a US evening at month end the default is still last month. The Volume Report and
  Prepaid Duties keep filtering on their local `date`/`ship_date` columns. Tests:
  `ReportLocalDatesTest` (bounds, DST, Rate Comparison, Packing Validation, Shipments by
  User) and three new cases in `ClientBillingReportTest` (a September 30 evening Label
  bills in September, the detail line and CSV date it locally, the default period by the
