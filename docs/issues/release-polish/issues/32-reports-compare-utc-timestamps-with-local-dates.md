# Client Billing and the other reports compare UTC timestamps with local calendar dates

## Problem

The filters compare `shipped_at`, stored in UTC, with bare local dates:
`>= 'Y-m-d'` and `<= 'Y-m-d 23:59:59'` (`ClientBillingReport.php:176-178`,
`UserShipmentsReport.php:111-112`). The Date columns show the UTC date
(`->dateTime('M j, Y')`). A label bought at 18:00 PDT on September 30 is
`2026-10-01 01:00` UTC, so it bills in October. Rate Comparison and Packing Validation
also drop the whole "until" day, because `shipped_at <= 'Y-m-d'` ends at midnight
(`RateComparison.php:109`, `PackingValidationReport.php:82,149,211`). The Volume Report
and the dashboard avoid this, because they key on `ship_date`, a local date.

## What to build

Convert the from/until bounds from the default Location's timezone to
UTC before comparing, and give the Date columns that timezone. Treat "until" as the end
of that local day everywhere.
