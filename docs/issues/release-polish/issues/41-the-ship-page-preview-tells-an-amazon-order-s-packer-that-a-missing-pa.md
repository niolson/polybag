# The Ship page preview tells an Amazon order's packer that a missing part number blocks the label, when Buy Shipping will buy it

> *This was generated by AI during release-polish pass 4.*

Status: ready-for-agent

Pass: 4 — bug (review of EU product identifiers)

Page or flow: Ship page, customs findings above the rates

Severity: low — EU-bound Amazon orders are off the demo path; misleading, not wrong at purchase

Verified: plausible. Line numbers are at `12592f33`; this area is unchanged on `dfd842c5`.

## Problem

`Ship::previewCustomsReadiness()` (`app/Filament/Pages/Ship.php:246-251`)
calls `CustomsReadiness::check($this->package)` with no Offer, so `$offerChannel` is null
and the Buy Shipping exemption (`CustomsReadiness.php:127-129`) never applies. The view
labels the finding "(blocks the label)" (`resources/views/filament/pages/ship.blade.php:204`).
At purchase the Buy Shipping Offer is exempt and the label is bought. The same preview
shows the origin and HS-code blocks for the same order. The duties-terms block was
reworded for this case (`international-customs-terms/05`, "worded as a refusal of direct
rates"); the identifier block was not.

## Evidence

Scenario: an Amazon-imported Shipment to a consumer in Germany (no company),
its Product with a SKU and no part number, the method allowing Amazon Buy Shipping. Open
`/ship/{id}`: "Product Identifier Required (blocks the label)" shows above a Buy Shipping
rate that buys successfully.

## What to build

When the Shipment's originating connection sells Buy Shipping, word
the identifier, origin and HS-code findings as applying to direct rates only, as the
terms block does.
