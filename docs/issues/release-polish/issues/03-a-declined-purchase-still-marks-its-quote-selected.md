# A declined purchase still marks its quote selected, so Rate Comparison reports a rate nobody bought

> *This was generated by AI during release-polish pass 4.*

Status: ready-for-agent

Pass: 4 — bug (review of label purchase and automation)

Page or flow: Ship page purchase, batch ship, and the Rate Comparison report (demo step 7)

Severity: medium — misstates the Rate Comparison report on the demo path. It affects
analytics, not billing.

Verified: confirmed. The test below fails at `12592f33`, and line numbers are at that
commit.

## Problem

`buyPostage()` calls `RateQuoteLogger::markSelected($offer)` as soon as it has inspected the
Offer (`app/Services/PackageShipping/EloquentPackageShippingWorkflow.php:513-515`). That is
before the report-printer check, the customs block, the claim, and the carrier's answer. So
a declined purchase, a customs refusal, a lost claim or a timeout leaves that quote row at
`selected = true`. When the packer then buys a different rate, the Package has two selected
rows.

`RateComparison` aggregates every quote row of the Package, across every quote session
(`app/Filament/Pages/Reports/RateComparison.php:55-62`, and `:130-134` for the savings
total):

- the price comes from `MAX(CASE WHEN selected …)`
- the carrier comes from a separate `MAX(CASE WHEN selected …)`
- the cheapest comes from `MIN(quoted_price)`

So the report can show the higher price, which nobody bought, beside an unrelated carrier
name, and count the difference as potential savings.

A void and re-buy (demo step 5) leaves two selected rows the same way, one of them for the
voided Label. A blind purchase has no selected row at all; that is already filed as
`postage-source-split/17`.

## What to build

- Mark the quote inside `recordBoughtLabel()`'s transaction, once the Label is written, so
  that only a recorded purchase is ever `selected`.
- For a void and re-buy, have the report read the selected quote of the active Label,
  through the Offer whose purchase that Label recorded, rather than any selected row.

Whether a voided Label's quote should stay `selected` is a product call. If the report reads
through the active Label, it is right whichever way that call goes.
