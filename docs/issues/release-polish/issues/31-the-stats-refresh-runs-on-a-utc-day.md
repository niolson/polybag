# The 15-minute rollup refresh runs on a UTC window, so the dashboard and Volume Report stop counting in the US afternoon and evening

> *This was generated by AI during release-polish pass 4.*

Status: ready-for-agent

Pass: 4 — bug (review of reports and billing)

Page or flow: Dashboard, Volume Report

Severity: medium — The dashboard is stale or frozen during US afternoon demos.

Verified: plausible. Line numbers are at `12592f33`; this area is unchanged on `dfd842c5`.

## Problem

`stats:aggregate --today` runs `everyFifteenMinutes()->between('6:00',
'22:00')` (`bootstrap/app.php:19-22`). The app and the scheduler are UTC
(`config/app.php:68`, no `schedule_timezone`), so the window is 02:00–18:00 EDT and
23:00–15:00 PDT. The full run is at 00:05 UTC, which is 20:05 EDT or 17:05 PDT.

- **A West Coast warehouse:** nothing bought after 15:00 PDT appears until 17:05.
- **An Eastern one:** nothing bought after 18:00 appears until 20:05. Labels bought after
  20:05 appear the next evening.

Even inside the window, "Shipped Today" lags up to 15 minutes. On `PackageShipped`,
`InvalidateDashboardCache` clears the widget caches but leaves the rollup row stale, so a
label just bought in the demo (step 3) does not move the dashboard.

## What to build

Run the `--today` refresh all day, or set the window in the default
Location's timezone. To make a purchase show at once, have the queued listener on
`PackageShipped` rebuild that Package's `ship_date`, the same listener the first finding
needs for voids.
