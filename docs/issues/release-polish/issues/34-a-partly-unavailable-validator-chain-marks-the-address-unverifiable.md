# A validator that was down later in the chain still leaves the address "Couldn't verify", and the schedule never retries it

> *This was generated by AI during release-polish pass 4.*

Status: ready-for-agent

Pass: 4 — bug (review of address validation routing)

Page or flow: scheduled validation (`shipments:validate`), Shipments list

Severity: medium — misstates validation on demo step 1 during any partial outage, and the paid validator that could settle the address is never asked again

Verified: confirmed. Line numbers are at `12592f33`; this area is unchanged on `dfd842c5`.

## Problem

`AddressValidationService::validate()` (`app/Services/AddressValidationService.php:43-82`) skips an unavailable validator with `continue` and leaves `$outcome` at the last answer. When an earlier validator was inconclusive and a later one was unavailable, the chain ends `Inconclusive`. The service then writes `deliverability = unverified` and sets `validation_attempted_at`. Under `03`, `unverified` means *every* validator was tried. Under `02`, a validator that could not be reached should be retried. Neither holds here: the Shipment reads "Couldn't verify", and `ValidateShipmentsCommand` (`app/Console/Commands/ValidateShipmentsCommand.php:34-36`) never selects it again. Typical cases are USPS 404 then a Google 5xx or broker outage, and FedEx inconclusive then a USPS 5xx. Only a manual Validate recovers it.

## What to build

Track whether any validator in the plan was unavailable. If one was and nothing settled the address, record the answers but not the attempt, so the schedule retries. The existing cap of 5 still bounds the paid calls. Leave deliverability at the provisional reading rather than `unverified`, or add a "check pending" state. Which of those to do is a product decision. Already-answered validators are asked again on the retry. That is the cost of this fix, and the cap bounds it.
