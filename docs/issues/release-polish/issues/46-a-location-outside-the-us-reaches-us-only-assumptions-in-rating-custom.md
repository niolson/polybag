## Problem

#331 lets a Location be created in the UK or anywhere else, and its
timezone now drives the ship date. The carrier paths it then reaches assume a US origin:

- USPS is still asked to rate: `UspsAdapter::buildRateApiRequest()` sends the postcode
  as `originZIPCode`, and `buildDomesticAddress()` would send `substr(postcode, 0, 5)`
  (`E1 6A`) as the sender's `ZIPCode`. Nothing excludes USPS for a non-US origin except
  unassigning it from the Location by hand, so every rate request shows a USPS error.
- Customs is declared in USD (`FedexAdapter` commodities, `UpsAdapter::buildCustomsDetail()`
  and `buildShipInvoiceLineTotal()`, `buildRateInvoiceLineTotal()`), and a product with no
  country of origin is declared made in `US` (`countryOfManufacture ?? 'US'`,
  `OriginCountryCode ?? 'US'`).
- Rates and label costs are read as plain numbers; a UK carrier account answers in GBP,
  and reports and client billing would add them to USD amounts.

## What to build

File as an issue rather than fix during the release: either state
that PolyBag ships from US Locations only (and keep #331's timezone and state changes for
territories and Canada/Mexico), or add an origin-country check that leaves USPS out of
rating for a non-US Location and a currency on the Location or carrier account. A
product decision.
