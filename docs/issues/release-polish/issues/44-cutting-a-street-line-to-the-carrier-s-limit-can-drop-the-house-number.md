# Cutting a street line to the carrier's limit can drop the house number

> *This was generated by AI during release-polish pass 4.*

Status: ready-for-agent

Pass: 4 — bug (review of label address characters and Locations outside the Americas)

Page or flow: Ship page purchase and batch ship, FedEx and USPS (and UPS once the previous finding cuts)

Severity: medium — the label prints a different, incomplete address with no warning; mostly international (extension), but long US lines with a unit number hit it too

Verified: plausible. Line numbers are at `12592f33`; this area is unchanged on `dfd842c5`.

## Problem

`FedexAdapter::buildStreetLines()` (`app/Services/Carriers/FedexAdapter.php`
line ~1632) keeps the first 35 characters of each street line and throws the rest away;
`UspsAdapter::buildDomesticAddress()` / `buildInternationalAddress()` (lines 2207, 2216,
2230, 2247) keep 50. In most of Europe and Latin America the house number comes after the
street name, so the tail is exactly what is lost. Transliteration makes this slightly
more likely, since `ß` → `ss` and Cyrillic `Щ` → `Shch` lengthen the line before the cut.
`AddressForm` allows 255 characters for `address1`, and imports are not limited at all.

## Evidence

Scenario: a FedEx International label to a Shipment whose `address1` is
`Avenida Doutor Francisco Sá Carneiro 1234` (40 characters) and `address2` empty. FedEx
receives `Avenida Doutor Francisco Sa Carneir` — no number — and the 2D barcode carries
the same. A US Shipment `12345 North Saint Clair Shores Boulevard Apt 1207` (49
characters) sent to FedEx loses `ulevard Apt 1207`, the unit included. Nothing tells the
packer; the Shipment and pack slip keep the full line.

## What to build

Product decision between: (a) wrap the overflow into the next free
street line (FedEx takes up to three, UPS three, USPS has `secondaryAddress`), and refuse
with a message naming the field only when it still does not fit; or (b) refuse the
purchase before anything is sent when a line exceeds the limit, so the operator
shortens it on the Shipment. Either way, never cut silently.
