# A parcel that was never put on the scale is bought at 0.05 lb

> *This was generated by AI during release-polish pass 4.*

Status: needs-triage

Pass: 4 — bug (review of the Pack page and box sizes)

Page or flow: Pack page, F12 / `%CSHIP`, manual and auto-ship

Severity: high — on the demo path (Pack, step 2), it buys postage at the wrong weight with no prompt, which misstates the Label and its cost

Verified: plausible (Alpine-only logic; no Pest test reaches it). Line numbers are at `12592f33`; this area is unchanged on `7649fe0c`.

## Problem

#392 added `usesMinimumWeight()` to `resources/views/filament/pages/pack.blade.php:376`:
when the scale is connected, its last reading was a stable `0.00`, and the weight field
still holds that reading, `effectiveWeight()` returns `minimumWeight` (`0.05`), so
`isReadyToShip()` passes and `ship()` sends `'0.05'` (`pack.blade.php:420`). A stable `0.00`
is also exactly what an empty platform reads. A packer who scans the box and items but has
not yet set the parcel on the scale (or set it on the bench) can press F12 and buy a label
at 0.05 lb. Before #392 a `0.00` weight blocked the button. The `%CZERO` command makes this
more likely: `zeroScale()` sets `weight` and `lastScaleWeight` to `'0.00'`
(`pack.blade.php:170`), and the next stable empty reading arms the minimum weight. Nothing
on the server catches it: `Measurements::hasPositiveValues()` accepts 0.05,
`Package::computeWeightMismatch()` (`app/Models/Package.php:750`) only records a flag, and
the declared-weight refusal exists for Shopify alone. With auto-ship on, the label is
bought and printed at once. The only signal is the small helper line "Too light for the
scale to read. Ships as 0.05 lbs." (`pack.blade.php:553`).

## Evidence

Scenario: scale connected and idle at a stable 0.00. Load a Shipment whose
item weighs 2 lb, scan a box (dimensions fill), scan the item, press F12 without weighing.
`isReadyToShip()` is true because `effectiveWeight()` is `'0.05'`; `ship()` saves a draft at
0.05 lb and buys at that weight (auto-ship) or quotes it on Ship. The carrier later bills
an adjustment.

## What to build

Product decision on how to tell "too light to read" from "not weighed".
Options: arm the minimum weight only after the reading has been non-zero since the Shipment
was loaded (something was placed and the scale settled back), or only when the packed
items' catalog weight is itself under the scale's resolution; and refuse, or ask once,
when `computeWeightMismatch()` is true at purchase, at least for auto-ship. A regression
test belongs in `tests/Browser` (pass 5), since the logic is Alpine.
