# Verify what the label endpoint accepts for a military destination

Status: needs-triage
Category: research
Type: manual

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md).

## Problem

Every military finding in the PRD came from the rate endpoint, which is generous: it
returns `PM` for Manhattan and a guaranteed Express date for Germany. The label
endpoint is where USPS actually says no, and nothing has been bought to a military
address on the sandbox since the customs-form fix.

## What to establish

One sandbox buy each, recorded here with the indicator and any error text:

- Priority Mail `PM` to an `AE` ZIP — accepted, and at the `PM` price
- Priority Mail `PM` to a domestic ZIP — expected to be rejected; the error text is what
  `01`'s refusal message should echo
- Priority Mail Express to an `AE` ZIP — accepted or not, and what the label says about
  a guarantee
- Ground Advantage to an `AP` ZIP with a parcel over the size limit USPS publishes for
  that post office — does the label endpoint enforce per-ZIP restrictions, or is that
  on us

And two things to look at without buying:

- What the Google and USPS address validators do with `PSC 1234 BOX 5678` / `UNIT 12345
  BOX 678` / `CMR 123 BOX 456` formats, which are the only forms a military address takes
- What the tracking endpoint returns for a military parcel after the gateway scan, and
  whether the existing delivered-to-agent handling reads it as delivered

## Recommendation

`ready-for-human` by the tracker's convention for sandbox work — though `05` showed the
sandbox is reachable from a session with `sandbox_mode` on and an EPS account, so this
could go to an agent with that caveat. The per-ZIP restriction question has no fixture
to test against and is the one that needs a person to read USPS's published list.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each buy above recorded with outcome, no tracking numbers or account identifiers
- [ ] Validator behaviour on the three address formats recorded
- [ ] Any rejection that `01` or `02` should handle is fed back into those issues
