# Deadline rules and the `MILITARY` commitment

Status: needs-triage
Category: bug
Repo: `polybag`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Parent

[PRD](../PRD.md).

## Problem

For an APO/FPO/DPO destination USPS quotes Priority Mail and Ground Advantage with
`commitment.name: "MILITARY"` and no delivery date at all — `scheduleDeliveryDate` is
absent, `guaranteedDelivery` is false. Priority Mail Express to the same ZIP comes back
with a date and `guaranteedDelivery: true` ("2 Days" to AE and AP).

`RateSelector::isOnTime()` counts a rate with no delivery date as missing the deadline.
So for a shipment with a delivery deadline going to a military address, every USPS rate
except Priority Mail Express is excluded, and automation buys the $52–$64 Express label
for a parcel that would have gone Priority Mail at $16. Nothing tells the operator that
this happened because USPS could not say when the parcel arrives, not because it would
have been late.

Whether the Express date is real is a second question. USPS's public description of
Priority Mail Express Military Service is a 2–3 day *estimate* to selected post offices
with no money-back guarantee, which does not match `guaranteedDelivery: true` from the
API. The Ship page shows the packer whatever USPS said.

## Desired behaviour

To be decided in triage; the options are not equivalent:

1. A dateless rate to a military destination is **not** a deadline failure — the
   deadline is treated as unknowable rather than missed, the rate stays eligible, and
   the selection reason says so.
2. Same as 1, and the Express "2 Days" is shown as an estimate for a military
   destination rather than a commitment, so a packer is not promised it.
3. Leave selection alone and surface it: the Ship page and the auto-ship log say a
   deadline could not be evaluated for this destination.

## Recommendation

`needs-info` from the maintainer on which option, then `ready-for-agent` — the
selection change is unit-testable against `RateSelector` with a fixture rate, and the
display change is a Filament page test. Nothing here needs USPS.
